New on every story · the "now" bar

One line that always says what's running

It sits at the top of the Pipeline panel and names the deepest step that's running, from the story's last event, with how long it has been going and the last sign of life. If nothing has happened for a while, it turns amber, so a stall can't hide behind a box that looks busy.

On a phone

A list you can scroll with your thumb, with the now bar pinned on top

On a phone, A and B come out the same: one column, a row per step. So the A/B pick only matters on a laptop. Three rules make it fit: the now bar stays pinned while you scroll, a finished lane folds to one row of chips, and only the running step opens up, with its inner steps listed under it. Tapping any row opens its trace in a sheet from the bottom, the same detail the laptop shows below the pipeline.
