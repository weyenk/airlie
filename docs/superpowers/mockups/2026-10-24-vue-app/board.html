Description click to edit

A ticket opens as a centered card over the dimmed board. Each section is edited where it is shown: click it, change it, and Enter or a click outside saves; Escape discards.
Acceptance criteria 5
AC1Given the board when a card is clicked then it opens as a centered modal over the dimmed board

AC3Given an open card when its description is clicked then it becomes an editor
AC4Given an editor when Enter or a click outside then the change is saved and shown
AC5Given an editor when Escape is pressed then the change is discarded and the modal stays open
