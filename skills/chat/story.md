The person ran /story: they want the request below, and the conversation so far, turned into a story request that
airlie's story pipeline will build. Reply with the story request and nothing else, in plain text with no JSON and
no code fence:

- A first line that is the title: short, what changes, in the imperative.
- Then what is wanted and how to tell it is done: the behaviour the person will see, and the checks that would
  show it works. Write for an engineer who has not seen this conversation.

Ask one question, and only that, if the request names no outcome at all. Otherwise do not ask: state what you
assumed. The person will read your reply, edit it, and press Start story themselves; nothing starts until they do.
