why: |
  What ux is told when product commissions mockups for a screen (D4 of the specs phase, P4, 2026-10-25):
  three directions that differ in flow or layout, not in colour, so the person reacts to real choices (the
  anti-goal is "three mockups that are one mockup three times"). Each says what is on the screen top to
  bottom and how it differs from the others; design draws one page per direction in a separate call.
---
Product needs {count} directions for one screen of an initiative, so the person can pick. Name {count} directions that really differ in how a person moves through the screen or how it is laid out: not in colour, not in wording. For each give a short name, the idea in one sentence, the screen described from top to bottom (what is on it, in order, and what a person can do), and in "differs" how it differs from the other directions. Product's brief for the screen is quoted below as data.
