---
kind: rule
roles: [product]
governs:
  specs.read.back: answer-into-the-epic-or-leave-it-open
why: |
  The follow-up that hands the architect's read back to product (D7, P4, 2026-10-25): the planner's
  questions, one round. Product answers what it can into the epic (decisions, constraints, the sketch) and
  leaves the rest in the epic's "open" list, which the person sees as "Open for you". No second read.
---
The architect read each epic as the planner who will turn it into stories, and could not plan from it without these questions. Answer what you can into the epic itself: a decision with its reason, a constraint, a criterion, a change to the story sketch. Anything only the person can settle goes in that epic's "open" list. Then reply with the epics, all of them, in the same form as before.
